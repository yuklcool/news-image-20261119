# THE WORLD TODAY — Thursday 19 November 2026

One image summing up the world news of Thursday 19 November 2026, drawn as a
newspaper front page. The program (`main.ts`) copies the pre-rendered
`entry.png` into the output directory; the page it was rendered from is kept
in `page/index.html` so the build is documented.

## The stories

1. **Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen** — the first-ever trilateral summit
   of the three nuclear powers, on the sidelines of APEC. Xi hugged Trump on
   the tarmac as he arrived.
2. **GTA VI launches worldwide** — Rockstar's sequel arrives 13 years after
   GTA V, scoring 99 out of 100 on Metacritic.
3. **US and Venezuela ink defence pact** — Defence Secretary Hegseth signs a
   deal in Caracas to re-establish military ties.
4. **World Court rules on Rohingya case** — the ICJ delivers its verdict in
   The Gambia's case accusing Myanmar of genocide against the Rohingya.
5. **Voyager 1, one light-day away** — NASA confirms that the 1977 probe is
   the first human-made object a light-day from Earth.
6. **Van Gogh sells for $190.4M** — "Blossoming Chestnut Trees" sets an
   artist record at Sotheby's after a deal with heirs over its Nazi-era sale.

All six stories happened or were first reported on 19 November 2026. Every
fact on the page was checked against news reporting from that day; nothing
was invented.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
# out/entry.png is a byte-identical copy of entry.png
```
